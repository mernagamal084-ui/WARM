The WARM story

Born on the sunniest day in Cairo

WARM began with a simple wish: carry something that feels like the first warm afternoon of spring. We design small-batch handbags in butter, peach, blush and sage, each finished with a hand-drawn garden that no one else will wear quite the same way.

Playful, never loud. Luxurious, never serious.

The art of illustration

A garden, drawn not printed

Every bag begins as a sketch in pencil, then ink, then watercolour-soft pigments sealed into the leather. Peach poppies, blue tulips and sage leaves are placed by hand, so each bag blooms slightly differently.

Materials and craftsmanship

Leather that feels like sunlight

Full-grain leather, gently pebbled, edge-painted and double-stitched. Handles are knotted by hand and hung on champagne-gold rings. Zips are smooth, feet are gold, linings are cream.

  • StitchingDouble, tonal
  • LiningSoft cotton twill
  • MadeSmall batches

Customer FAQs

Good to know

How long does delivery take?

Greater Cairo and Giza: 2–4 business days. Other governorates: 3–6 business days.

How much is shipping?

EGP 85 flat across Egypt, free on orders from EGP 5,000.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes — cash on delivery is the only option in this demo, and it is simulated.

Are the illustrations identical on every bag?

Each design follows the same sketch, but because it is painted by hand, small differences make every bag unique.

How do I care for my bag?

Wipe with a soft dry cloth, store stuffed with tissue, and keep away from direct heat and water.
